Install NYT Games

  1. On the Supernote: Settings → Security & Privacy → turn on Sideloading.
  2. Plug the Supernote into this computer with its USB cable.
  3. Click Install NYT Games, pick the Supernote in the box that opens, and tap Allow on the Supernote if it asks.
  4. On the Supernote: open NYT Games and sign in to your NYT account (menu ☰ → Log in).

Use a Chrome-based or Edge browser on a desktop computer to install. Your login and puzzles are kept when you update.

You need your own NYT Games subscription for the games that require one. This app is unofficial. It isn't made by or connected to The New York Times.

For other install methods, and the code, see the README.
